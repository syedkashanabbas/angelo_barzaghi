A Lifetime of Screen Printing Excellence

Angelo Barzaghi is an internationally recognized screen printing expert, consultant, educator, and author. With over 30 years of dedicated experience in the screen printing industry, Angelo has built a reputation of trust and expertise that transcends borders.
